---------------------------------------------------------------- PROMPT 1
I ship candles through USPS from a Google Sheet. One tab, Packages, has Order, Service,
Length, Width, Height (inches) and Weight (pounds) for every package. Another tab, Rates, is my
own rate card: Service, Pounds, Price. Write me an Apps Script with a menu that works out the
dimensional weight of each package, tells me which ones are being billed for more than they
weigh, and how much extra that costs me on my rate card.

---------------------------------------------------------------- PROMPT 2
That version disagrees with USPS. Here is the rule, from Postal Bulletin 22705, in force since
July 12, 2026. Rebuild it to follow this exactly:
- Round each side UP to the next whole inch before anything else. An exact whole number stays.
- Multiply the three rounded sides. If the result is MORE than 1,728 cubic inches, divide it by
  139 and round up to the next whole pound. That is the dimensional weight. At 1,728 or less
  there is no dimensional weight.
- Round the real weight up to the next whole pound too. USPS bills the bigger of the two.
- It applies to Priority Mail Express, Priority Mail, Ground Advantage and Parcel Select. The
  cubic-priced services use their own tiers: set those aside and say so.
- Refuse any row you cannot read, with its row number and the reason. Never guess a size.
- Price the difference from my Rates tab only. If a weight is missing from my card, say so.
- Add a Boxes tab with my box sizes and tell me which of my boxes trigger dimensional weight.
Then I'll check every number against a separate checker written from these rules.

---------------------------------------------------------------- PROMPT 3
The checker disagreed with you on 23 packages. Fix the three causes: round each side, not the
volume; "more than 1,728" means a 12-inch cube is NOT dimensional; and the cubic services are set
aside, not priced by this rule. Then make the sheet check itself before I trust it:
- Find every column by its header, in any order. If a tab's header row is missing or wrong,
  check nothing on that tab and say which tab.
- Count in public: packages in = checked + set aside + refused, every time.
- Add up the extra money twice, two different ways, and compare to the cent.
- If any of that fails, or more than 10% of the rows are refused, write
  DO NOT TRUST THESE WEIGHTS across the top of the report, in red, and leave it there.
- For each package billed above its weight, name the largest box from my list that is smaller
  than the one I used and would not be billed above that package's weight. Say it cannot know
  whether the candles fit.
